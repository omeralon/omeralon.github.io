## The Monster Project, Jerusalem 2019

Kids in the 5th grade imagined, wrote and drew monsters, then artists recreated them in their own styles. The goal is to encourage children to dream, create and believe in their abilities, while improving their writing and reading skills. Instructor: Zeev Engelmayer. Photo by: John Theodore.

<Media src="/work/etc/Monster.jpg" size="medium" />
